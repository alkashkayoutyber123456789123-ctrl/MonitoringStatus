📋 System Architecture

• Physics Model: Non-paralyzable dead-time equation with $\tau = 190\ \mu\text{s}$ and J305 calibrated sensitivity ($0.00812\ \mu\text{Sv/h/CPM}$).

• Auto-EVA Detector: Activates under $450\text{ hPa}$, dynamically tightening dose alert limits for unshielded space.

• Space Weather: Direct NASA DONKI API integration for early solar proton storm warnings.
